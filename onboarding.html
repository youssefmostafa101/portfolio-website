I only need Klaviyo access to do 95% of the work. Here's how to add me:

  1. 1Log into your Klaviyo account
  2. 2Click your account name (top right) → Settings
  3. 3Go to Users & Permissions
  4. 4Click Invite User
  5. 5Enter my email: yusef@cliyra.com

Shopify access (Month 1 only)

I only need Shopify access during onboarding — to grab product images, set up discount codes, and make sure the Klaviyo integration is firing correctly. After that, I work entirely inside Klaviyo.

  3. 3Click Users and Permissions

CHAPTER 06

The partnership.

What makes this work, and what doesn't.

SECTION 6.1

What I expect from you

This works best when we're both doing our part.

  • ✓ Respond to my messages within 24 hours during the week
  • ✓ If I need an approval, give it — delays cost us both
  • ✓ When I ask for assets, send them. If you don't have them, tell me
  • ✓ Trust the system. Email is a long game. Flows compound over months, not days
  • ✓ If something's wrong, tell me directly. I'd rather fix a problem than lose a client

SECTION 6.2

What you can expect from me

  • ✓ I respond during my working hours. Every day. No exceptions
  • ✓ Your emails go out on time, every time
  • ✓ I track your KPIs monthly and tell you exactly what's working and what's not
  • ✓ I'll never send an email I wouldn't be proud to show you
  • ✓ If I think something's off with your strategy, I'll tell you — even if it's not what you want to hear
  • ✓ I treat your brand like my own

SECTION 6.3

What kills partnerships

Not calling this out to scare you — just being upfront.

  • Slow approvals. If I'm waiting on you every week, the work suffers.
  • Changing direction constantly. I need at least 60–90 days to show real results.
  • Ghosting. If something's wrong, talk to me. We can fix almost anything.
  • Expecting overnight results. Flows take 30–60 days to collect data and optimize.
